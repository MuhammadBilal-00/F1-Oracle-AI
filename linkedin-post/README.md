## Video vs carousel — my take

**A short video/GIF wins for this project.** It's an interactive, animated dashboard — motion proves it's real and built, not mocked. Best single asset: a **20–35s screen recording** that:

1. Lands on the Overview, 2. opens ⌘K and jumps to the Simulator, 3. runs a simulation (the animated leaderboard is the "wow"), 4. flips to Predictions (SHAP) and the Research Lab clustering.

LinkedIn natively autoplays video in-feed, which lifts dwell time and reach. **Use the carousel (these 8 images) as the fallback / second post.** Either way, lead with a visual — a text-only post about a *visual* product underperforms.

Record with the Windows Game Bar (Win+G) or ShareX; keep it under 40s, no audio needed.
